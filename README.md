🌊 KYL — Know Your Lakes
A smart beach and lake safety platform built to help families, swimmers, and beachgoers stay informed, safe, and prepared in Michigan.

KYL helps users make safer decisions before visiting a lake or beach by combining real-time safety insights, accessibility support, crowd awareness, and environmental alerts into one simple platform.

🚀 Inspiration

Many people visit Michigan lakes without knowing important safety risks like:

dangerous rip currents
unsafe bacteria levels
overcrowded beaches
inaccessible locations
child safety concerns

Our team wanted to create a platform that makes lake safety easy, accessible, and proactive.

✨ Features
🌊 Rip Current & Safety Alerts
Uses weather and lake condition APIs to warn users about:
- dangerous swim conditions
- rip current risk
- strong winds
- unsafe wave levels

👥 Crowd Meter
Shows how busy a beach is using:
- user check-ins
- time-based estimates
- future real-time WebSocket updates

♿ Accessible Beach Finder
Helps users locate beaches with:
- wheelchair access
- ramps
- accessible parking

🦠 Lake Bacteria Alerts
Displays water quality and bacteria warnings so users can avoid unsafe swimming areas.
