Understanding E. Coli Levels

Safe (0-235 CFU/100ml)

Water quality is within a normal safe range for recreation.

Advisory (236-400 CFU/100ml)

Elevated levels. Sensitive groups should avoid swimming.

Closed (400+ CFU/100ml)

Unsafe levels. Swimming should be avoided until retesting.
